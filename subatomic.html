Subatomic Particles

Introduction

All matter is made of atoms, and atoms themselves are composed of even smaller particles called subatomic particles.

  • Chemical reactions involve the rearrangement of electrons
  • Ions form when atoms gain or lose electrons
  • Isotopes affect atomic mass and nuclear stability
  • Bonding and reactivity depend on valence electrons
