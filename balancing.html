Balancing Chemical Equations

Introduction

Balancing chemical equations demonstrates the Law of Conservation of Mass: atoms are neither created nor destroyed during chemical reactions.

Law of Conservation of Mass

In a balanced chemical equation, each element has the same number of atoms on both the reactant and product sides.
