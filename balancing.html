Balancing Chemical Equations

Introduction

Balancing chemical equations helps us show the validity of the conservation of mass: that matter is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. This skill is foundational for stoichiometry, reaction analysis, and laboratory work.

Law of Conservation of Mass

The Law of Conservation of Mass was first proposed by Antoine Lavoisier. It states that the total mass of a closed system remains constant over time. This law holds true in chemical reactions under normal conditions (20–25°C, 1 atm). Chemical reactions only involve electrons and do not change atomic identities. The law does not apply to nuclear reactions, where mass can convert to energy.
